Internship Program

Work on real manufacturing automation projects — not toy datasets or tutorial apps. Blue Vector interns work directly with clients, robots, and production environments.

What We Do

Blue Vector helps manufacturers reduce waste and automate repetitive tasks using collaborative robots and machine vision systems. Interns join active client projects and contribute to real deployments — from writing robot programs to analyzing production data to building dashboards that supervisors actually use.

🤖

Robotics Integration

Program and deploy Standard Bots RO1 collaborative robots in real manufacturing environments. Learn machine tending, pick-and-place, and cobot safety practices.

👁

Machine Vision

Set up and tune vision systems for quality inspection and robot guidance. Work with camera hardware, lighting, and image processing software on live production lines.

📊

Process Analysis

Apply kaizen and lean manufacturing principles to identify waste in real workflows. Build measurement systems, map value streams, and recommend improvements.

⚙️

Systems & Software

Write tools, dashboards, and integrations that connect machines to data. Experience ranges from Python scripts to embedded systems depending on the project.

Program Details

What to expect

  • Hands-on work from week one — no busywork projects
  • Direct mentorship from Blue Vector engineers
  • Exposure to real client environments (machine shops, fabrication, assembly)
  • Work with industry hardware: cobots, vision cameras, PLCs
  • Deliverables you can show in a portfolio or thesis

Who we're looking for

  • Engineering students (mechanical, electrical, industrial, computer science)
  • Curious — willing to get on the shop floor and figure things out
  • Basic programming experience (Python preferred, not required)
  • Available for at least 10 hours/week; full-time summer positions available

We're happy to provide a formal internship agreement, learning objectives, and midterm/final evaluations compatible with most engineering co-op programs. Reach out and we'll send over whatever your department needs.

How to Apply

Send an email introducing yourself. Tell us what you're studying, when you're available, and what kind of work interests you most. No formal cover letter needed — just be direct about what you want to learn and what you can bring.

Attach a resume or link to any relevant work (GitHub, portfolio, class projects). We review applications on a rolling basis.
